Mahall History (1968 – 2026)

58 Years of Dedicated Service

In the monsoon of 1968, forty devout families gathered under the visionary guidance of late Haji K.V. Mohammed Koya and Marhum Sayyid Alavi Thangal to establish the first thatched prayer hall on ancestral land donated along the coast of Calicut. The objective was clear: create an enduring institutional sanctuary where prayers, moral education, and social relief could flourish hand-in-hand.

Over six decades of unwavering community sacrifice, that humble prayer shed transformed into a sprawling four-acre educational and spiritual campus. By 1982, the primary Madrasa was established, securing board affiliation with the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. In 1996, the Central Juma Masjid was reconstructed in grand Kerala-Mughal architectural harmony, accommodating over 2,500 worshippers simultaneously.

Today, in 2026, Noorul Huda Mahall Jama'ath stands as an exemplary smart digital Mahall in Kerala, combining digital household census, solar-powered infrastructure, computerized welfare trusts, and free 24/7 dialysis and ambulance desks.

Core Philosophy

Vision & Mission Statement

Our Vision

"To be an exemplary Islamic Mahall community grounded in pure Sunnah principles, distinguished by social justice, poverty eradication, holistic academic excellence, and progressive digital transparency for future generations."

Faith • Unity • Compassion • Transparency

Our Mission Pillars

  • Spiritual Sanctity: Foster congregational prayers, genuine love for Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and Quranic literacy across all age groups.
  • Social Safety Net: Zero destitute hunger policy through monthly food security kits, free dialysis, and emergency medical grants.
  • Youth Empowerment: Guide young scholars into higher civil services, technological innovation, and ethical leadership.
  • Amicable Sulhu: Preserve matrimonial and civil harmony through confidential, impartial Islamic arbitration before civil litigation.
