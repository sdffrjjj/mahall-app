Islamic Programs

Weekly Friday Post-Jumu'ah Dars

Imam Nawawi's 40 Hadith Commentary

Every Friday immediately following the Jumu'ah prayer, Chief Imam Usthad Abdul Rasheed Faizy explains one Hadith with modern daily life applications for youth and professionals.

Duration: 20 Minutes • Central Prayer Hall
Monthly Qiyam-ul-Layl & Tahajjud

Spiritual Night Vigils

Conducted on the last Saturday night of every Hijri month from 03:30 AM to Fajr prayer. Includes congregational Tahajjud, Istighfar litanies, and collective Suhoor meal.

Free Suhoor meal provided to all attendees
Divine Word

Qur'an Programs & Circles

Annual Hifdh Sanad Convocation

Honoring young Huffaz who have completed memorization of the Holy Qur'an with traditional Isnad chains tracing back to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Tajweed Masterclasses

Weekly intensive practical training in Makharij-ul-Huroof and Sifaat led by Hafiz Salmanul Farisi for university students and adults.

Sisters' Online Zoom Circle

Morning daily recitation and word-by-word Quranic meaning circles exclusively for mothers and sisters at 10:00 AM on Zoom.

NHYM Wing

Youth Programs & Leadership

Emergency Rescue Volunteer Brigade (NH-RVB)

Trained corps of 45 youth volunteers certified in coastal water rescue, first aid CPR, disaster relief, and emergency crowd management during Eid and Jumu'ah.

Inter-Ward Badminton & Football League

Annual winter sports tournament held at beach sports grounds promoting brotherhood and fitness among young men across Wards 1 to 8.

Children's Programs

Prophetic Moral Storytelling

Sunday morning sessions for children aged 5 to 10 narrating inspiring stories of Prophets, Companions, and righteous role models.

Arabic Calligraphy Workshops

Hands-on weekend workshops introducing children to the beauty of Thuluth and Naskh scripts using traditional reed pens and inks.

Junior Islamic Quiz League

Interactive buzzer quiz competitions assessing general Islamic knowledge, Seerah, world geography, and Quranic trivia.
