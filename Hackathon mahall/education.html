Islamic Academy

Noorul Huda Madrasa Academy

Estd 1982 • Reg #104

Noorul Huda Islamic Academy is affiliated with the renowned Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. Over 44 years of educational service, the academy has educated generations of scholars, community leaders, and ethical professionals.

The curriculum integrates traditional Qur'an recitation with modern digital learning tools, moral ethics, Arabic communication, and English supplementary coursework. With a pupil-teacher ratio of 34:1, every child receives personal attention in Tajweed articulation and character development.

Message from Sadar Muallim (Headmaster):

"Our supreme aspiration is to instill unshakeable Taqwa, brilliant moral character, and authentic Islamic scholarship in every student, preparing them to excel in both this Dunya and the Akhirah."
— Usthad K.V. Hamza Faizy (Sadar Muallim)
