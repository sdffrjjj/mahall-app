Knowledge Center

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do new residents register their household under Noorul Huda Mahall?

New residents should submit the Mahall Family Registration Form along with an NOC from their previous Mahall, proof of residence (ration card/electricity bill), and Aadhaar copies of all household members. The Ward Convener conducts verification within 3 business days.

What are the requirements for conducting a Nikah at the Central Mosque?

The bride's Wali must apply for a Marriage NOC at least 7 days prior. Both parties must submit age proof (minimum 18 for bride, 21 for groom) and attend the complimentary pre-marital counseling session with Chief Imam Usthad Abdul Rasheed Faizy.

How does the Sulhu (Arbitration Council) handle family or civil disputes?

The Sulhu desk is completely confidential. Submissions are read exclusively by Chief Imam and two appointed neutral arbitrators. Over 94% of cases (marital disputes, boundary issues, inheritance settlements) are amicably resolved without civil litigation.

How are destitute families selected for the monthly ration kit scheme?

Beneficiaries are audited annually by ward social audit panels focusing on widows, chronic dialysis patients, and destitute households without regular income. Total transparency is maintained with independent Waqf auditing.
