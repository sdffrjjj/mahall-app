Education 01 • Samastha Board

Noorul Huda Islamic Academy (Madrasa & Dars)

Academic Excellence with Islamic Character

Founded in 1974, Noorul Huda Madrasa provides systematic Islamic education from Class 1 to Class 12, following the accredited syllabus of the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. We cultivate ethical values, Quranic comprehension, Arabic grammar, and contemporary life skills.

Primary Batch Classes 1 to 4 Basic Tajweed & Akhlaq
Secondary Batch Classes 5 to 7 Fiqh, Thareekh & Hadith
Higher Secondary Classes 8 to 12 Samastha Board Exams
Timings: Morning 06:30 AM - 08:30 AM (Mon - Sat)
Hifdh Batch: Evening 04:30 PM - 06:00 PM
