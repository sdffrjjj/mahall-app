Graceful towers overlooking the Calicut coastline, fitted with specialized directional loudspeakers calibrated to avoid disturbance.

Intricate white Makrana marble niche adorned with Quranic Ayatul Kursi calligraphy carved by master craftsmen.

Acoustic Teakwood Ceilings

Traditional Kerala timber craft ensuring natural acoustic resonance and optimal climate control even during peak monsoons.
