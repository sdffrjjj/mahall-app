SERMON FOCUS FOR THIS FRIDAY

"Compassion in Community Living & Mutual Respect"

Delivered by Moulavi Usthad Abdul Rasheed Faizy (Chief Imam & Khateeb). The Khutbah explores Islamic neighborhood rights, peaceful conflict resolution, caring for elders, and maintaining communal brotherhood in urban spaces.

1. Standard Congregation Timings

1st Adhan 12:15 PM Courtyard Open
2nd Adhan 12:45 PM Minbar Call
Khutbah 12:50 PM Bilingual
Fardh Salah 01:15 PM Congregational

2. Sunnah Etiquettes & Worshipper Conduct

• Ghusl & White Attire: It is strongly recommended to take a full ritual bath (Ghusl), clip nails, wear clean modest white clothing, and apply non-alcoholic perfume (Itr).
• Early Arrival & Tahiyyatul Masjid: Arrive before the 2nd Adhan. Offer 2 Raka'ahs of Tahiyyatul Masjid even if the Imam has started speaking.
• Absolute Silence during Khutbah: Do not speak, whisper, or signal others once the Khateeb ascends the Minbar. Mobile phones must be powered off or placed in flight mode.
• Courteous Seating: Do not cross over people's shoulders or separate two worshippers already seated. Fill front rows first in compact lines.
• Surah Al-Kahf & Salawat: Recite Surah Al-Kahf during Friday daylight hours and multiply blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ.

3. Parking, Traffic & Women's Facilities

• Vehicle Parking: Four-wheelers must park at East Ground Parking (Gate 3). Two-wheelers park at Courtyard North. No parking in front of Gate 1 ambulance corridor.
• Women's Sanctuary: 1st Floor North Wing is accessible via Gate 2 ramp. Dedicated wudhu plaza, nursing room, and high-clarity sound system available.
• Elderly & Differently-Abled: Ground Floor Hall A features wheelchair ramps, special wudhu seating with handrails, and elevator access to upper levels.
